copad is the phone and tablet client for comux, the terminal multiplexer built for running AI coding agents. Connect to your own computer over SSH and everything comux shows — sessions, tabs, split panes, agents and their notifications — appears as a native app, not a squeezed-down terminal screen.

SEE YOUR AGENTS
• Every agent across your sessions, with what it is doing and how long it has been at it
• Agents waiting for you stand out, one tap away
• Notifications when an agent finishes or needs an answer, end-to-end encrypted: only your device can read them

A REAL TERMINAL
• Full-colour terminal with Nerd Font glyphs and scrollback
• Korean and other input methods compose properly before anything is sent
• A key bar for Esc, Ctrl, Tab, arrows and the comux prefix, and hardware keyboard shortcuts

COMUX, NATIVELY
• Switch sessions, open tabs, rename, jump between panes and agents from native menus
• The session picker, directory browser and prompts are native sheets

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your terminal connects straight to your machine over SSH; only encrypted notifications pass through our push relay
• Log in with a key kept in your device's Secure Enclave, or a password kept in its Keychain
• No account, no analytics, no ads

copad needs a computer running comux (free and open source: https://copad.marshallku.dev) that your phone can reach, for example over Tailscale.
